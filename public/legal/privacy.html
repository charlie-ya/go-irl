Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

Welcome to Roamin' Empire! This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data when you use our mobile and web applications.

1. Information We Collect

To provide you with our location-based gameplay, we collect and store the following information:

  • Location Data: Roamin' Empire relies heavily on geolocation tracking to function. We collect your precise GPS location while the app is actively running in the foreground to map your position and allow you to claim territory. We do not track your location in the background.
  • Account Information: If you sign in using Google Auth or Apple Sign-In, we securely receive and store your identifying email and account ID.
  • Profile Data: We store your chosen "Explorer Name" and map color to customize your map rendering.

2. How We Use Information

We use your data exclusively to:

  • Operate the game's core interactive map layer.
  • Identify territory ownership between completely anonymous players.
  • Process in-app purchases.

3. Data Visibility & Safety

Your "Explorer Name" and map color are public and visible to other users who encounter the territories you have claimed. Your real name, email address, physical location history, and exact device telemetry are entirely strictly private and never published.

4. Account Deletion & Data Retention

You may request the complete deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time from within the Profile Menu of the application using the "Delete Account" button. Executing this permanently scrubs your account ID, balance, and historical claims.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our support team.
